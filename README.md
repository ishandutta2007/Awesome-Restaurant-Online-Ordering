# Awesome-Restaurant-Online-Ordering

## Top Restaurant Online Ordering Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Direct Online Ordering, Branded Websites & Apps, Pickup/Delivery, Menu Management, POS Integration & Commission-Free Ordering*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Restaurant Online Ordering**. These systems let restaurants accept orders for pickup and delivery through their own branded website or app, manage menus, process payments, and send tickets to the kitchen—often with the goal of reducing reliance on third-party marketplace commissions.



**Examples** include ChowNow, GloriaFood, Owner.com, Flipdish, Deliverect, Lunchbox, Otter, Square Online, Toast Online Ordering, and Menufy (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured, multi-location online ordering platforms with marketing tools, advanced delivery integrations, and polished guest experiences are mostly commercial. There is, however, a useful open-source ecosystem of restaurant POS + online ordering systems (**URY**, **KitchenAsty**, **OpenEats**, and others) that support self-hosted direct ordering. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ChowNow](https://get.chownow.com/)**  

  Popular commission-free direct online ordering platform for independent restaurants, with branded ordering, marketing tools, and POS integrations.



- **[Toast Online Ordering](https://www.toasttab.com/)**  

  Online ordering tightly integrated with the Toast restaurant POS ecosystem, covering website and app ordering for pickup and delivery.



- **[Square Online](https://squareup.com/online)**  

  Accessible online ordering and website tools that integrate with Square POS, suitable for restaurants wanting a simple, low-cost direct channel.



- **[GloriaFood](https://www.gloriafood.com/)**  

  Well-known online ordering solution (now part of a larger portfolio) that offered free and paid tiers for web and QR-code ordering.



- **[Owner.com, Flipdish, Menufy](https://www.owner.com/)**  

  Platforms focused on branded restaurant websites, online ordering, and guest engagement for independent and multi-location operators.



- **[Deliverect, Otter, Lunchbox](https://www.deliverect.com/)**  

  Solutions that specialize in order aggregation, channel management, and integrating marketplace + direct orders into kitchen and POS workflows.



- **[Other restaurant online ordering platforms](https://get.chownow.com/)**  

  Additional commercial tools covering white-label apps, loyalty-linked ordering, and multi-location direct ordering.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[URY – Open Source Restaurant Management](https://github.com/ury-erp/ury)**  

  FOSS restaurant management system built on ERPNext. Includes POS supporting dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and aggregator-style orders, plus kitchen display and operational tools.



- **[KitchenAsty](https://github.com/mighty840/kitchenasty)**  

  Self-hosted restaurant online ordering, table reservation, and management system. Supports delivery/pickup ordering, menu management, real-time order tracking, kitchen display, and admin operations.



- **[OpenEats](https://github.com/BenJonesVA/OpenEats)**  

  Self-hosted restaurant POS system with order counter, kitchen display, table floor plan, checkout, and sales dashboard. Runs on a single machine via Docker with no cloud dependency.



- **[NutrixPOS](https://github.com/nutrixpos/pos)**  

  Free open-source restaurant and retail management system with REST API architecture for inventory, sales, and product management.



- **[Ordering Management System examples](https://github.com/phojie/ordering-management-system)**  

  Open-source restaurant/ordering management systems with customer-facing ordering, admin dashboards, kitchen roles, and delivery-related workflows.



- **[URY POS](https://github.com/ury-erp/pos)**  

  Web-based order management component (part of the URY ecosystem) supporting dine-in, delivery, and takeout order types.



- **[Other restaurant POS & ordering projects](https://github.com/search?q=restaurant+online+ordering+OR+restaurant+POS+open+source)**  

  Additional community POS, menu, and online-ordering implementations.



- **[Headless commerce adapted for restaurants](https://github.com/search?q=headless+commerce+OR+food+ordering+open+source)**  

  Open commerce platforms that teams customize for restaurant menus, modifiers, and fulfillment flows.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Menu & modifier engines**: Open models for complex restaurant menus, options, and pricing rules.

- **Kitchen display / KDS**: Standalone open KDS projects that can receive orders from a custom online frontend.

- **Payment integrations**: Stripe, Adyen, or other open SDKs for accepting online payments.

- **Delivery partner connectors**: Community scripts that push orders to third-party delivery providers or internal drivers.

- **QR-code & table ordering**: Lightweight open tools for scan-to-order experiences.

- Self-hosted stacks combining KitchenAsty or URY with a custom storefront and payment provider.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

The strongest open-source starting points for direct online ordering are **KitchenAsty** (purpose-built online ordering + reservations + management) and **URY** (full restaurant ERP/POS with delivery and takeout support).  

**OpenEats** and **NutrixPOS** provide solid self-hosted POS and order-management foundations.  

These can be paired with custom storefronts, payment gateways, and delivery integrations.  

Commercial platforms (ChowNow, Toast Online Ordering, Square Online, Owner.com, Flipdish, Menufy, Deliverect, Otter, etc.) deliver polished guest experiences, marketing tools, multi-location management, and reliable support that most self-hosted solutions still require significant effort to match.  

Many independent restaurants use a commercial direct-ordering platform alongside their POS; tech-comfortable operators sometimes run fully self-hosted stacks for maximum control and zero per-order commissions.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Online ordering systems handle payments, personal data, and order accuracy. PCI compliance, data protection, food-safety processes, and reliable kitchen integration are the operator’s responsibility.

- Open-source ordering platforms offer freedom from commissions and full data ownership but require hosting, security hardening, payment compliance, and ongoing maintenance. Evaluate total cost of ownership and operational readiness carefully before production use.
